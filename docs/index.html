01 · THE CHALLENGE

Where should decision-makers focus limited resources?

Rapid urban development creates overlapping environmental pressures. When information is spread across separate datasets, comparing districts and identifying priority areas becomes difficult.

02 · THE APPROACH

From fragmented datasets to comparable insights.

I integrated population, land-use, transportation, and vegetation data; engineered district-level indicators; constructed a composite risk index; and examined spatial patterns with statistical methods.

03 · THE INSIGHTS

Make patterns visible—and explain why they matter.

The analysis reveals meaningful differences across districts and relationships among development pressure and vegetation. An interactive map and research charts will be added here in the next lessons.

Interactive map coming in Lesson 4

04 · RECOMMENDATIONS

Translate analysis into better decisions.

Organizations could use district-level risk patterns to identify locations for further assessment, prioritize potential interventions, and monitor environmental indicators over time. These are proposed uses of the research, not measured intervention outcomes.
