# Addis Ababa, Ethiopia: Sustainability Risk Assessment
## Project Overview
This study develops a composite environmental sustainability risk index for urban zones in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and assesses the socioeconomic factors that impact woreda (district) level risk index.

Environmental sustainability risk is defined as urban sustainability stressors that increase vulnerability to environmental hazards and limit access to natural resources and green space. The index is developed using five key indicators: population density, road density, transportation density, built-up percentage, and inverted normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI).

## Research Objectives
- Develop a composite index to assess environmental sustainability risk.
- Examine spatial differences in environmental conditions across Addis Ababa.
- Identify relationships among environmental and socioeconomic indicators.
- Provide evidence to inform sustainable urban planning and policy decisions.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- GeoPandas
- Geospatial data analysis and visualization

## Sustainability Risk Indicators
- Population density
- Road density
- Built-up area percentage
- Transportation density
- Mean Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)
